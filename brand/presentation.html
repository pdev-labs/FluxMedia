What we heard

The brief

An open-source media downloader and library for desktop, web and Android. The identity should feel fluid, fast, clean and capable — a tool for people who collect and keep media, not another streaming service.

fluidfastcleancapableopen

Distinct from generic play buttons, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, and keeps the blue/cyan equity of the previous mark.

FluxMedia — final identity2
Final design

Flux Swoosh

A play triangle whose lower edge lifts into a current, with a detached cyan wake.

  • Reads as media first and FluxMedia second — the right order for an app icon.
  • The detached wake keeps the flow idea from the old logo without the gradient.
  • Two flat shapes and one curve survive 16 px, one colour and embroidery.
